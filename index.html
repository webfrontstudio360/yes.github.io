Comfort, company and care close at hand

Retirement should feel like coming home. At Anistemi Aftree-Oord, residents have their own space, friendly people around them and help available the moment they need it.

Whether you're looking for an independent suite today or thinking ahead about the care you or a loved one may need later, you can stay in one familiar community as your needs change. Harmony Hill Assisted Living & Frail Care is on site.

Families are welcome to come and see the village for themselves. Call us or send an enquiry and we'll arrange a visit.

  • Independent retirement suitesYour own suite and your own routine, in a safe, friendly village.

  • Dedicated caregiversA caring team who get to know each resident.

Care that grows with you

Move in at the level that suits you now. If your needs change, the support is already here.

  1. Independent retirement suites

    Live in your own suite with the freedom to run your day your way, with a community and help close by.

  2. Assisted living

    A little help with everyday tasks where it's needed, while you keep your independence and routine.

  3. Clinical care

    Clinical and nursing support on site for residents who need regular medical attention.

  4. Frail care

    Closer day-to-day support from dedicated caregivers, including Harmony Hill Assisted Living & Frail Care on site.
